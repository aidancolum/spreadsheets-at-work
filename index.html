Practice files for Spreadsheets at Work

These five workbooks go with the book Spreadsheets at Work by Aidan Colum. Download them, save a copy of each one before you change anything, and work on the copy.

The files

  • orders_export_RAW.xlsx
    The inherited file: 240 rows of orders straight from a system export, with a merged title and several planted problems. Used in Chapters 1 to 5.
  • monthly_summary_solution.xlsx
    The 228 clean orders as a table, plus a Summary sheet to check your own work against. Used from Chapter 2 on, and in Chapters 6 and 9 to 14.
  • price_list.xlsx
    Twenty products, SKU-1001 to SKU-1020, for lookups. Used in Chapter 8.
  • employees_roster.xlsx
    Forty-eight employees and the sales reps, for comparing two lists. Used in Chapter 7.
  • badge_access_list.xlsx
    Forty-six names with badge numbers, to compare with the roster. Used in Chapters 4 and 7.

About the data

The company is Harbor & Pine Supply Co. It does not exist. All people, customers, companies and amounts in these files are invented for teaching. Any likeness to a real business or person is a coincidence.

The files are teaching material only. They contain no macros. If a file opens in Protected View, choose to enable editing on your copy.
